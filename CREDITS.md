# Credits

Hapstack adapts pstack by Lauren Tan (poteto), published by Cursor under the MIT license. Most of its top-level skills, its 24 engineering principles, its playbooks, guide, agent briefs, and helper sources come from pstack with portable adaptations. Hapstack has since removed some pstack skills (arena, swarm, make-bot-ui), the dormant Benny automation pack, and the orchestration and autopilot playbooks with their helpers, and moved the principles into hap-mode reference files. The source revision is recorded in UPSTREAM; LICENSE preserves its copyright notice.

The deslop skill is adapted from cursor-team-kit in the same source repository and revision. The control-ui/control-cli adapters, portable setup/runtime contract, routing, and public patcher/ship adaptations are Hapstack contributions. Patcher's Improve reference credits the evidence-based approach in shadcn/improve; it does not bundle that external package or inherit its delegation behavior.

The Full send playbook and the PR filing and babysit rules adapt tstack by Theo Browne, published under the MIT license. Its full-send, full-stop, file-pr, and babysit-pr skills are folded into hap-mode playbooks rather than shipped as separate skills. The source revision is recorded in UPSTREAM.

Hapstack is independent and is not endorsed by Cursor, Lauren Tan, Theo Browne, or the original pstack or tstack projects.
